# Director's Sandbox — Team Spark

**Tagline:** Prompt a world, then direct the movie inside it.

## Elevator pitch
World models can *generate* a place — but you can only look at it. Director's Sandbox
turns a generated world into a film set. Type a prompt, and World Labs' Marble builds an
explorable 3D world as your backdrop. Then you *direct* it in real time — moods, cinematic
cameras, and a story timeline. Hit ▶ Play Scene and our AI auto-director turns your single
prompt into a full cinematic arc with camera cuts, a procedural score, and adaptive
narration. Record it to a clip in one click. From one line of text to a directed,
watchable scene — in about a minute.

## What it does
- **Generate** a World Labs Marble world from text; it drops in as a live 360° backdrop.
- **Direct** live: mood commands (Golden Hour, Red Alert, Alien Contact, Ruins, Downpour),
  cinematic cameras (Wide, Close-Up, Orbit, Drone, POV), and a story-beat timeline.
- **AI Auto-Director** composes a prompt-aware cinematic arc and plays it back.
- **Procedural soundscape** (Web Audio) + **voice narration** (SpeechSynthesis).
- **In-app recording** to a downloadable clip.

## Built with
next.js, react, typescript, three.js, world-labs-marble-api, web-audio-api,
